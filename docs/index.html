Chimera

A new move for your creature.

Chimera turns a 3D model and a text prompt into editable skeletal animation. LTX-2.5 generates a motion video. Chimera fits the model's own skeleton to it and exports GLB, FBX and BVH.

This page shows real exports from the app, and nothing runs in your browser. Each result was chosen by eye from the takes we generated, so this is the best of the output. Run the app itself from the repository.

Chimera hero: generated by the author with Tripo, then edited and given a custom rig. Desk lamp: original model for this project.

Brute, lizard and spider: asset 3dJsSjUpXHBpMX394X4ycyxQ, asset AA2zEeGqUkuact5KQT3Qxd95 and asset mbNRB9MAHUpAu6N7pkyrn9Bn by Scenario, CC BY 4.0. Rigged with SkinTokens and animated by Chimera.
